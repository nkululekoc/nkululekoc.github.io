How I approach the work

The same method, every project.

Whichever stage a business starts at, the work moves through the same three phases.

01

Discover & diagnose

Understand your business, customers, competitors and current digital ecosystem — then identify the gaps and highest-impact opportunities.

02

Design & deploy

Develop the strategy and digital architecture, then implement the systems, campaigns, platforms and experiences.

03

Measure & accelerate

Track performance against real business objectives, then optimise, automate and scale what's working.

Where you start

Three stages, depending on where your business is today.

Digital transformation isn't a single project — it's a progression. Most businesses fit somewhere on this path already.

Stage 01 — Digital Step

Establish your foundation

Websites, Google Business, social channels, digital branding and the infrastructure behind it all — we'll get your business digitally ready.

For: startups, SMEs and businesses with an underdeveloped digital presence.

Stage 02 — Digital Growth

Turn presence into growth

Social, paid media, search, content and community working together as a system for generating attention, leads and customers.

For: businesses with a presence that isn't yet generating enough customers.

Stage 03 — Digital Acceleration

Scale what's working

Marketing transformation, WhatsApp automation, analytics and campaign management — the capabilities your next stage of growth needs.

For: established businesses ready for deeper digital transformation.

FAQs

How do we get started?

With a Digital Transformation Assessment — a straightforward look at where your digital presence stands today, and which of the three stages fits your business right now.

I don't know where my business sits on the path — is that a problem?

No — that's the norm, not the exception. Most businesses have a mix: solid in one channel, missing in another. The assessment is built to work that out with you, not to expect you to already know.

Do you build the WhatsApp automation yourself?

Yes. WhatsApp automation and chatbot strategy is the hands-on work I'm currently building for clients directly, from strategy through to deployment.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the stage and scope — a foundation project moves faster than a full growth or acceleration engagement. Timelines are scoped after the assessment, once we know exactly what's needed.

Do you work with businesses outside South Africa?

My track record is built on South African retail and FMCG brands, but the framework isn't market-specific. Book a call and we can talk through your context.
