How I think about this

A few of the ideas that shape how I work with clients. More on the way as the consultancy grows.

Digital transformation doesn't start with technology

It starts with getting the fundamentals right — a working website, a claimed Google profile, a clear message. Most "transformation" projects fail because they skip this step.

Presence isn't performance

Having a Facebook page, an Instagram account and a website isn't the same as having channels that actually bring in customers. That gap is where most of the work lives.

WhatsApp is becoming South Africa's front door

For a lot of local businesses, WhatsApp already is the customer service channel — it's just running without a system behind it. That's usually the fastest place to start.
